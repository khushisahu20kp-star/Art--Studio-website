Our Process

From concept to creation — how each piece comes to life

01

Consultation

We begin with a conversation about your space, color palette, and vision. Together, we define the perfect piece for your interior.

02

Material Preparation

Premium plaster, natural pigments, and hand-stretched canvas are selected and prepared with precision.

03

Texturing & Layering

Using trowels, spatulas, and custom tools, each layer is applied by hand. This stage takes 3–7 days depending on complexity.

04

Gold Leaf & Finishing

Delicate gold leaf is applied where desired, followed by sealing and protective coating for lasting beauty.

05

Delivery & Installation

Each piece is carefully packaged and delivered. We offer professional installation services for larger works.
