About The Artist

The story behind every texture

Elena Vasquez

Founder & Lead Artist

With over 12 years of experience in textured art, Elena founded Aurelia Studio to bring warmth and depth to modern interiors. Her work draws inspiration from Mediterranean architecture, natural stone formations, and the subtle beauty of weathered surfaces.

Each piece is handcrafted using traditional plaster techniques combined with contemporary aesthetics. No two works are ever the same — every stroke, every layer tells a unique story.

The Creative Process

01. Material Selection

Premium plaster, canvas, and natural pigments sourced ethically.

02. Layering

Each piece is built up in thin layers, allowing drying time between applications.

03. Finishing

Gold leaf, sealing, and final detailing to perfect the texture.
